Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Synology Backup" Google OAuth application. This application is a strictly private, single-user tool created for personal NAS data backup purposes.

2. Data Collection and Usage

This application does not collect, share, or monetize any user data. It exists solely to obtain an OAuth token allowing a personal Synology NAS to upload and manage personal backup archives on the owner's Google Drive account.

3. Third-Party Access

No third parties have access to this application, its authentication tokens, or the data it transfers. It is locked to a single authorized developer account.

4. Contact

As this is a private application, public support is not provided. The developer can be contacted at the email address registered in the Google Cloud Console.
